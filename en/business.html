Potash

Potash

Core business

Potassium is one of the three primary crop nutrients, with nitrogen and phosphorus. Saskatchewan sits on large, deep potash deposits, and the potash produced there is mainly potassium chloride.

About 30% of the world's potash is produced in Canada.Source: U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (2025 estimate)

Fishmeal

Supply chain

Canadian potash travels mainly by rail to Vancouver and to Portland, Oregon, on the west coast, and to Thunder Bay and Saint John in the east, where it is loaded onto ships. The railways and terminals are run by third parties; we do not name suppliers on this website.
